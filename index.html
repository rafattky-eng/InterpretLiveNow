How to use

Talk with anyone in English or Japanese — no typing needed.

  • 1 Tap Record and speak in your language
  • 2 Wait for the translation to appear
  • 3 Tap Speak so the other person hears it
  • 4 Hand the phone over — they record their reply
  • 5 Keep going back and forth
