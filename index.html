How to use

Talk with anyone in English or Japanese.

  • 1 Choose EN or JP (language you will speak)
  • 2 Tap Record → speak → tap Stop
  • 3 Or type / paste text and tap Translate
  • 4 Tap Speak so the other person hears the translation
  • 5 Hand the phone over and continue
